Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 24 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124).

A vintage broadsheet, **"The World Today"**, summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one 1024×1024 image:

1. **Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO** — shares leap 31.5%, adding $952 billion in a day.
2. **U.S. blacklists Megaspeed** — the Singapore cloud firm and six affiliates join the Entity List over alleged Nvidia chip diversion to China.
3. **26 dead in the Cikande candy factory fire** — the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia blaze in Serang, Indonesia; eight still missing.
4. **Ukraine sinks the Russian corvette Merkury off Libya** — seven sea drones in Benghazi harbour; Zelensky urges four-party peace talks.
5. **Russian Kh-101 missiles land in Poland** — three came down on 20 November; Warsaw calls it a provocation.
6. **Car bomb and drone attacks rock Colombia** — 23 wounded in Villavicencio, three civilians wounded in Cúcuta.

All six stories were verified against Reuters, AP, CNN, Le Monde and WSJ before drawing. The image is rendered by `render.py` (PIL, no network) and `main.ts` simply copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, deterministically — two runs produce byte-identical output.
